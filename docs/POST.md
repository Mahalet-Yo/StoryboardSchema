# Public post: notes for writing it yourself

The brief asks for a post *written by you*: the story, why it matters to you, and one thing you learned. Turn the platform's AI label on before publishing. These are prompts, not a post.

- **The story in one line:** a daughter washes her father's feet and receives his *mirkat*, and his blessing outlives him.
- **Why it matters to you:** [a parent's or grandparent's blessing you remember; who you'd want to watch it].
- **One thing you learned**, pick one that is true for you:
  - an image filter refused a gentle family scene until the shot was replanned as hands and water;
  - the suggested ducking setting beat your own guess once you measured it;
  - the most important line, the father's blessing, needed 11.5 seconds, so the narration ended up setting the edit.
